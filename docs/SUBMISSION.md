# Submission preparation — Lunar Landing Site Navigator

This project responds through terrain-aware geometric Earth visibility, sunlight windows, interactive lunar site exploration and two-site tradeoffs. It does not yet evaluate all landing safety criteria.

The following are owner-provided submission constraints for NASA Space Apps 2026; they have not been independently established as current official rules in this development stage. Verify current organizer requirements before submission. Keep the official demo to **no more than 30 seconds of video OR 7 slides**.

Prepare public, accessible links to the repository, demo, methods, NASA source citations and any slides/video. Check links while signed out; provide captions/transcript for video, readable contrast and text explanations of charts. Credit NASA LRO/LOLA, GSFC PGDA, Barker et al. (2023) and NASA JPL Horizons. Disclose the pre-event prototype and subsequent work without claiming unsupported organizer permission.

Evaluation goals supplied by the owner:

- Impact: show one concrete landing-site exploration decision and the mission tradeoff.
- Creativity: explain the combined terrain, Earth and sunlight geometry in an understandable map workflow.
- Validity: show the tested observer/time model, data versions and visible limits.
- Relevance: connect each demo result directly to Lunar Landing Site Navigator.
- Presentation: use one reproducible scenario, legible results and concise narration within the format limit.
