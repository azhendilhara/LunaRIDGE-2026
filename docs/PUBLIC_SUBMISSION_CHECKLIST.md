## Submission content

Explain the Lunar Landing Site Navigator response: select South Pole coordinates/dates, compute terrain-aware Earth/Sun geometry, assess editable requirements, compare scenarios and explore a bounded candidate set. Credit NASA Horizons and LOLA separately, with Barker et al. paper/data DOIs.

Show actual computed values with exact UTC bounds, mast, sampling, radius and coverage. Retain Haworth sensitivity. Missing coverage is not zero. Finite-window darkness is not permanent shadow, sunlight is not electricity/battery charge and Earth geometry is not RF connectivity, landing safety or mission-success probability. Defaults are illustrative, not NASA recommendations. Candidate preference is not a universal best site. Do not imply NASA endorsement/sponsorship/certification.

Use the supplied judging goals—Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation—to explain usefulness, creativity, evidence, scope and clarity. Keep methods, validation, credits and accessible submission materials available.
