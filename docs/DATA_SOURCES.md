# NASA data sources, verification and usage terms

## LOLA terrain

Source: NASA LRO Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA), NASA GSFC Planetary Geodesy Data Archive (PGDA), [A New View of the Lunar South Pole from LOLA](https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/90).

Required attribution requested by the archive: **Barker et al. (2023), The Planetary Science Journal, 4, 183**, [paper DOI 10.3847/PSJ/acf3e1](https://doi.org/10.3847/PSJ/acf3e1); [data DOI 10.60903/gsfcpgda-lola-spole](https://doi.org/10.60903/gsfcpgda-lola-spole).

Download [LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ.TIF](https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_20mpp/LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ.TIF) using `backend/download_dem.py`. The downloader verifies the fingerprint and raster metadata before moving its `.part` file to the final destination. Existing files are verified rather than accepted based on size.

From the project root:

```powershell
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/download_dem.py
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/download_dem.py --verify-only
Get-FileHash data/lunar_dem/LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ.TIF -Algorithm SHA256
```

Pinned prototype fingerprint:

```text
File: data/lunar_dem/LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ.TIF
Bytes: 189158392
SHA256: 82030dcbdd7c4bb0c7d858e9b4b293b9f2cda5a543ba31ec0f42784bd6beee5a
Raster: 7600 x 7600, one float32 band, 80 m cells
Bounds: x,y each -304000 to +304000 m
NoData: NaN
Projection: lunar south polar stereographic, lat_ts=-90, radius 1737400 m
Archive frame: MOON_ME, JPL DE421
```

This SHA-256 is a locally computed reproducibility fingerprint of the preserved dataset, **not a NASA-published or signed checksum**. A future NASA revision may legitimately have different bytes. Review its provenance and metadata explicitly before changing this pin. Do not bypass verification by accepting only a matching file size. The verifier opens the raster and checks metadata plus a small read; this is not an independent scientific validation of every pixel.

The PNG `frontend/public/lunar_south_pole_dem.png` is a derived terrain visualization. Credit NASA LRO/LOLA, NASA GSFC PGDA and Barker et al.; preserve these credits in exports and demos. Raw DEM data are excluded from GitHub and must be obtained separately.

## JPL Horizons

NASA JPL Solar System Dynamics [Horizons system](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/), [API documentation](https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/horizons.html), and [scientific manual](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/manual.html).

The application queries Earth (399) and Sun (10), observed from the Moon (`coord@301`), with UTC observer ephemerides and airless apparent azimuth/elevation (quantity 4). Retain scenario dates, sampling and observer height with any shared result. Live service results can evolve with NASA model updates; the application does not pin or redistribute an ephemeris kernel.

## Terms and licenses

The PGDA product page supplies a data-use citation policy; no separate Creative Commons license is asserted here. Preserve its requested paper/data citations. NASA's [media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) explain that NASA-created content is generally not subject to US copyright, while NASA identifiers, third-party material and endorsement have separate restrictions. NASA credits are attribution, not a claim of NASA sponsorship or certification. Consult the linked current terms when preparing public materials.

Application code has no newly assigned license in this stage. Dependencies retain their own licenses in installed distributions. Review source and asset rights before selecting a repository license; in particular, the provenance of `frontend/src/assets/hero.png` has not been independently established. No rights to third-party assets are inferred from NASA's policy.

## Independent verification references

[LROC QuickMap](https://quickmap.lroc.asu.edu/) and [NASA Moon Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/) can help inspect terrain and locations. They are references, not runtime data integrations in this application. [Canadian Space Agency Moon exploration](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/astronomy/moon-exploration/) is an educational reference, not an implemented dataset source.
