Both upstream font repositories carry SIL Open Font License 1.1: [Plus Jakarta Sans](https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans/blob/master/OFL.txt), [JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono/blob/master/OFL.txt).
